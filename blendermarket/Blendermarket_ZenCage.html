Transform and arrange objects with one intuitive cage

Zen Cage wraps your selection in an interactive bounding box for precise alignment, fitting, distribution, arrays, clamping, rotation, and scaling directly in the 3D View.

Core workflows

Align

Align objects or components to a cage point, edge, face, axis, or outside boundary.

Fit

Fit a selection inside or outside the cage with uniform or per-axis scaling.

Distribute

Create even spacing with margins, fixed distances, and cage-aware placement.

Array

Duplicate selections along X, Y, or Z and adjust count and spacing in real time.

Clamp

Scale objects to a cage boundary while locking the chosen minimum or maximum side.

Rotate

Rotate around cage points or the 3D Cursor with an interactive dial and fixed angles.

Scale

Scale uniformly or per axis through cage handles in global or local orientation.

Flexible Cage Setup

Choose Dynamic, Locked, or Manual bounds to match quick edits and repeatable layouts.
