Explore Zen Masters Add-ons

Organize, select, and visualize anything in Blender

Zen Sets turns mesh elements, objects, and collections into reusable named groups. Recall complex selections, manage large scenes, and see every group clearly in the 3D Viewport and UV Editor.

01

Save complex selections

Store vertices, edges, faces, objects, and collections as named groups you can recall at any time.

02

Control large scenes

Select, hide, isolate, sort, rename, and restructure groups without fighting the Outliner.

03

Understand structure instantly

Display groups by color in the 3D Viewport and UV Editor to read complex models at a glance.

Core workflows

Group Selector

Select, hide, isolate, and switch groups directly on the model with the Workspace Tool.

Object Groups

Organize and recall related objects without changing Blender's native collection structure.

Collection Control

Navigate, select, hide, isolate, and manage nested collections from the 3D Viewport.

Color Display

Visualize mesh groups by color in both the 3D Viewport and UV Editor.
